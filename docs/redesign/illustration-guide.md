# Illustration guide

Every painted background on the site comes from the OpenAI image API (`gpt-image-2`, edit endpoint). Each call sends one **style block**, one **scene line**, and the matching band of Kim An's mock as the reference image. Change the style block only with Kim An's sign-off. When it changes, regenerate every asset, so the set stays one style.

## Style block (verbatim)

> Painterly digital landscape for the background of a calm Vietnamese astrology website. Soft gouache and airbrush texture, gentle film grain, layered mountain ridges fading in atmospheric perspective, fine gold star specks and thin gold constellation lines used sparingly. East Asian mood, cinematic but quiet. Palette: night #0e1222, deep blue #1b2a4a, dusk violet #6b3a4d, ember orange #d9623b, dawn gold #f2c879, cream #f7f2e7, gold #f2b441. Match the painting style, light and colours of the reference image, but paint only the landscape: no text, no letters, no numbers, no buttons, no user interface, no logos, no people, no borders, no frames.

## Con giáp emblems

The tile emblems do not use the style block, because it asks for a landscape. Prompt (verbatim), with the Dần emblem as the reference for the other eleven:

> A single round emblem centred on a perfectly flat, plain, solid deep navy background (#0e1222) that fills the whole square edge to edge: no landscape, no sky, no mountains, no clouds, no stars outside the ring, no texture, no vignette. Inside a thin gold ring with twelve small gold star points evenly spaced on it, the animal is drawn in fine gold line art with a little soft gold shading, exactly matching the line style, gold colour, ring, stars, size and placement of the reference image; only the animal changes. The emblem fills about 80 percent of the square. No text, no letters, no numbers, no frame, no border. The animal is _the animal_.

Use the Vietnamese zodiac: Sửu is a water buffalo, Mão is a cat, Thìn is an East Asian dragon without wings. After export, the navy background is recoloured to `night` (`magick -fuzz 7% -fill '#0e1222' -opaque 'srgb(3,17,47)'`) so it matches the tile.

## Saving calls on small assets

Assets shown at 128 px or less (emblems, icons, small thumbnails) are generated as one grid: ask for an evenly spaced grid (for example 4×3 in 1536×1024) with plain background between cells, then crop each cell with `magick -crop`. One call replaces twelve, and the set comes out in one consistent style. Paintings shown full width are still one per call.

## Output and delivery

- Generate at 1536×1024 for desktop and 1024×1536 for mobile. A6 is 1024×1024.
- Export as WebP at 768 and 1536 px wide (`magick -resize <w>x -quality 70`). Budget: 120 KB at desktop size and 60 KB at mobile size.
- Shipped so far: A1 (variant 1) is 45 KB / 13 KB, A1b is 92 KB / 21 KB, A2 (variant 2) is 69 KB / 21 KB, A3 (variant 2) is 52 KB / 15 KB, A4 (variant 1) is 53 KB / 17 KB, A5 (variant 2) is 93 KB / 29 KB, A6 (variant 1) is 47 KB at 800 px / 13 KB at 400 px, and L1 (variant 1) is 47 KB / 13 KB. Chapter 5 has no painting of its own: a cream, dawn, cream gradient joins chapter 4's haze to chapter 6.
- Lá số: the cung emblems (L2) are 2.5–4 KB at 128 px / 7–13 KB at 256 px, the ngũ hành icons (L3) are about 1.5 KB at 64 px, and the board night (L4) is 20 KB / 4 KB, brightened with `magick -modulate 175,120` after Kim An asked for a lighter backdrop.
- Tết hub: the hub hero (T1 v2) is 75 KB / 23 KB, the twelve con giáp heroes are 60–80 KB / 17–23 KB, and the emblems are 3–5 KB at 128 px.
- Share cards for the 74 hub pages are not generated by AI: `pnpm nx run @org/frontend:tet-hub-og` sets the title over the page's painting with the site fonts and writes `public/tet/og/<slug>.jpg` (1200×630, about 68 KB).
- A painting behind a hero that does not fill the viewport becomes the LCP element, because Chrome only skips images that cover the whole viewport. Moving it to a CSS or `::before` background, or raising its fetch priority, does not change that. So the Tết hub heroes show their painting from 768 px only, as a lazy `<img>` that phones never fetch, and phones keep the plain Night band (measured 2026-11-13).
- Paintings are decoration. Use them as CSS backgrounds, or as `<img alt="">`, and never put text inside them.
- The chapter keeps its flat tone colour underneath, so it reads the same before the painting arrives.
- Add one painting per PR. Measure five production Lighthouse runs on `/` before and after it ships.
