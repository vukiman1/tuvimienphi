# Redesign research — market, SEO and design references

Collected 2026-11-03. Traffic figures are Similarweb public estimates for the three months to September 2026; they are desktop-biased and only good for ranking sites against each other. Keyword volumes were not available; anything marked _guess_ has no data behind it.

## Audience

From Kim An: young to middle-aged Vietnamese, visiting in their free time. Two motives that pull the design in different directions:

- **Believers** come for "this year's hạn", lucky days and hours. They need the site to feel serious and trustworthy.
- **Self-discovery seekers** treat the lá số like a deep personality test. They leave if the site feels like cheap superstition.

Both read at leisure, so the luận giải itself is the product and has to read like a good magazine piece.

## Gaps we can own

1. **Trust.** Incumbents carry lottery/casino links, clickbait headlines, sold banners and stale year labels. No ads, no clickbait, a stated privacy policy and honest pricing stand out on sight.
2. **Lá số UX.** Everyone ships the same dropdown form, a static chart image and a wall of text. Nobody has an interactive, mobile-first board that explains a star or palace on tap.
3. **Readable readings.** Nobody splits the reading into chapters, summarises before going deep, or links a statement back to the star that produced it.
4. **Honest pricing.** Clear per-chapter credit pricing with a free chapter beats fake discounts and phone-number walls.
5. **Freshness.** Several sites still headline 2024; nobody has a Tết 2027 hub yet.

## SEO demand

Evidenced:

- **"Âm lịch hôm nay" / "lịch âm"** is the volume king: daily, repeat traffic, owned by entrenched calendar sites.
- **"Lá số tử vi", "lập lá số tử vi", "lasotuvi"** belong to tuvi.vn, cohoc and tracuutuvi, largely through branded navigational searches.
- **"Xem ngày tốt xấu", "ngày hoàng đạo"** belong to xemngay.com and lichngaytot.
- **Per-age × year pages win.** ngaydep.com has `/nam-2027-nam-sinh-nam-1987-sao-gi-chieu-menh.html` for every gender × birth year. Others use `tu-vi-tuoi-{can-chi}-nam-{year}-nam-mang-{birthyear}`.
- **Retail blogs (MoMo, Shopee, FPT Shop, Điện Máy Xanh…) rank for the same article queries** with far more authority, so pure article SEO is a losing fight.
- **Tết searches start about three months ahead** ([VnEconomy, Feb 2026](https://en.vneconomy.vn/google-reveals-tet-2026-trends-vietnamese-consumers-leverage-ai-for-smarter-celebrations.htm)). "Ngày tốt đầu năm" and "văn khấn mùng 1 Tết" were top Tết trends ([VietNamNet](https://vietnamnet.vn/tet-tan-suu-nguoi-viet-dua-nhau-tim-van-khan-xem-ngay-tot-i277161.html)). "Tuổi xông nhà" recurs every year.
- **People already paste charts into ChatGPT** (FPT Shop and Phong Vũ publish prompt guides), so demand for AI readings is real.

Guesses: "tử vi 2027", "sao hạn 2027", "tam tai 2027", "xông đất 2027" and "xem tuổi vợ chồng" peak between mid-December and Rằm tháng Giêng (around 2027-02-20). "Cúng sao giải hạn" peaks the week after Tết.

## Ranked opportunities

1. **Tết 2027 per-age hub, live by early December.** One page per birth year × gender, generated by our own engine (sao chiếu mệnh, hạn, tam tai, Tứ hoá lưu niên), each ending in "lập lá số" → chapter 06 Vận hạn.
2. **The free interactive lá số as the hero, AI chapters as the honest paid upgrade.**
3. **A fast, ad-free "Hôm nay" page** (âm lịch, giờ hoàng đạo, ngày tốt/xấu) for retention and repeat visits rather than cold SEO.

## Design references

What separates crafted from AI-generic in this genre, from Co–Star, CHANI, The Pattern, Nebula's 2026 rebrand, DaoWay, OWSPACE 单向历 and Japan's 72 Seasons:

- **One signature device used consistently**, instead of every mystic cliché at once.
- **Ornament where it matters** (the date, the chart), not on every card.
- **Borrow a real artefact** rather than invent a pastiche. OWSPACE rebuilt the 黄历 almanac with modern type and became "the young people's old huangli".
- **Verdict first, depth on demand**: a short Nên / Kiêng, then "go deeper".
- **One illustration hand.** Mixed stock illustration is the clearest AI tell.

Vietnamese references: lịch bloc and lịch vạn niên print, Marou chocolate by Rice Creative (gold used once, as a print ink), Đông Hồ and Hàng Trống woodblock prints, hand-painted shop signs.

What they share: son red as the lead colour on cream paper or a red field, gold for borders and ornament only, very bold poster-style display type, and one reusable kit (tường vân clouds, hồi văn fret borders, round frames, ribbon banners, seals) plus flat cut-out illustrations. They avoid the AI look through flat fills, clear outlines and one consistent hand, not through having less ornament.

Vietnamese type notes: test stacked marks (ấ ề ổ ữ ặ) and the horn (ơ ư) in real copy, avoid uppercase with tight leading, and avoid thin display serifs at small sizes. A Vietnamese subset on Google Fonts means the glyphs exist, not that they are well drawn.
